## Roles

- Strategist: prioritizes campaigns, approves calendar
- Writer: drafts posts, ensures voice consistency
- Producer: captures screenshots/clips, ensures alt text
- Reviewer: quick sanity check (accuracy + tone)
- Scheduler: schedules posts, tracks performance

Note: On a small team, one person may wear multiple hats.

## Rituals

- Weekly 15m: pick 1–2 stories for the week
- Monthly 25m: review metrics, decide next campaign

## Definition of Ready
- Clear outcome, target audience, asset plan, owner

## Definition of Done
- Post published with alt text + UTM, recorded in calendar
